# ICP

## Primary Audience

Men ages 18-24 who understand the basics of trading but are not sophisticated or professional traders. They want to trade with the structure and confidence of a professional desk, without having to build the full process themselves.

## Core User Profile

This user knows what a ticker, chart, entry, stop loss, and take profit are, but does not reliably know how to connect market context, catalysts, technical location, and risk/reward into one disciplined trade. He is not looking for a dense professional terminal. He wants the product to spoon-feed the process, surface the right checks, and make him feel like he is participating in a serious trade decision.

He is likely influenced by social trading content, WallStreetBets-style momentum, YouTube/Twitter traders, and big macro narratives. He may overtrade, chase moves, enter late, ignore invalidation, or confuse a good story with a good trade. The product should help him slow down just enough to avoid obvious mistakes while still feeling fast and exciting.

## Product Implications

- Mobile-first experience.
- Fast, visual, tap-first decisions.
- One decision at a time to reduce cognitive load.
- Agent should feel proactive and opinionated, but the user confirms the trade direction and final setup.
- Copy should feel direct, confident, and trader-native, but should avoid assuming professional knowledge.
- Explain the reason for each check in plain language without sounding like a lesson or survey.
- Visuals should prioritize charts, key levels, probability, risk/reward, and setup cards over dashboard-style portfolio views.
- Demo flows should emphasize momentum, catalysts, retail attention, macro backdrop, and technical location.
- The product should do most of the work: gather the backdrop, identify why the ticker matters, map levels, flag bad location, and suggest a setup.
- The user should make simple consequential choices: ticker, direction, whether the location is clean, and whether to trade/edit.
- Avoid feature density, advanced dashboards, portfolio analytics, or pro-terminal complexity unless it directly supports building one trade setup.

## Current Positioning

The product is a guided trade setup builder for young retail traders who know the basics and want to trade more like professionals, without needing professional-level market knowledge.
